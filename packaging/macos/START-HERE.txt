Athan Time for macOS 15 or later

Read README.txt beside the app for first-run settings, updates, removal and support.

1. For a ZIP, extract it completely using Archive Utility. For a DMG, open the image.
2. Drag Athan Time.app into Applications, then double-click it.
3. You can also double-click Launch-Athan-Time.command beside the extracted app.
   After the app opens, you can close Terminal.

Choose Apple Silicon for an M-series Mac, or Intel for an Intel Mac.
The .NET runtime is included. Open the app itself to start it.
Keep the app's contents together.

To update, fully quit Athan Time (including its menu-bar icon) before replacing
the app in the same location. Your per-user settings are kept. If you move the app,
disable and re-enable Start on login so it uses the new location.

If permissions were lost during extraction, run this once from the extracted folder:
  bash Launch-Athan-Time.command

This beta is unsigned and not notarized. macOS may require approval through
System Settings > Privacy & Security. Follow your organization's security policy.
If it fails, send the exact error and your macOS version to athantimedotnet@gmail.com.

Project: https://github.com/Cophla/AthanTime.Net
Downloads: https://github.com/Cophla/AthanTime.Net/releases

First run uses OS language/time format and suggests a city; confirm or change it.
Settings are kept when you remove the app. To remove your settings too, fully exit
the app and run Remove-My-Settings.command as your normal user BEFORE deleting the app.
Type REMOVE to confirm. Do not use sudo. Other users and custom recordings are kept.
