# Athan Time v1.0.2-beta.16

## Release description

Athan Time shows prayer times and plays Athan reminders on Windows, Mac and
Linux. It works offline and needs no account.

### What’s new

- **Movement breaks.** Turn on occasional reminders to stretch gently, walk, or rest your eyes. Choose the timing and add your own ideas.
- **Reading highlights.** Follow a moving highlight in text Adhkar and movement reminders. Change its speed in Settings → General.
- **One place for backups.** General now opens first. Back up the entire app or choose one section, including your movement ideas.
- **A fresh start when you need it.** Restore factory settings clears your local customizations and closes the app. Your recordings and exported backups are kept.
- **Desktop improvements.** City search text is easier to read, and Linux login startup requests minimization after the window opens.

### Getting started

Choose the download for your computer. On Windows x64, run Setup or extract the
Portable ZIP. On Mac, choose Intel or Apple Silicon and open Athan Time.app
after extraction. macOS 15 or newer is required. On Linux, choose x64 or ARM64,
extract the ZIP, and use the included launcher. The downloads include .NET.

Confirm your city and keep the app running with your computer awake to receive
reminders. This is a beta. Windows and Mac may show security warnings when you
open the app.
